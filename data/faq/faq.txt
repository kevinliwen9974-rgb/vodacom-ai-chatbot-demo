# Vodacom FAQ knowledge base
# Refreshed 2026-10-24 from official Vodacom Help and Support pages.

Question1: Which price plan would suit me?
Answer1: Powerflex: Month-to-month, no contract or credit check. Choose 7GB, 17GB, 20GB or 22GB Anytime data with minutes and SMS. Change or cancel anytime. Prepaid plans include Power Pack (double data) and NXT LVL (youth social bundles). Vodacom RED bundles airtime, data and VodaPay rewards. SIM-only plans give data, minutes and texts without a handset.
Source1: https://www.vodacom.co.za/vodacom/help/switch-to-vodacom

Question2: I've switched to Vodacom but why can't I connect to the network yet?
Answer2: If your port was approved today, your number will be ported between 19:30 and 23:30 tonight. If still not connected, visit a Vodacom Shop or call Customer Care on 082 135.
Source2: https://www.vodacom.co.za/vodacom/help/switch-to-vodacom

Question3: How to port (switch) a Contract number to Vodacom?
Answer3: Visit a Vodacom Shop with your ID or passport, proof of residence, 3 months’ bank statements (stamped), and 3 months’ payslips. We’ll do a credit check and request port approval from your current provider. Once approved, sign your new contract and receive your Vodacom SIM and equipment.
Source3: https://www.vodacom.co.za/vodacom/help/switch-to-vodacom

Question4: How long will it take to port (switch) my cellphone number?
Answer4: Ports are processed on weekdays (excluding public holidays) between 19:30 and 23:30. You can choose same-day porting or a date up to 30 days ahead.
Source4: https://www.vodacom.co.za/vodacom/help/switch-to-vodacom

Question5: How do I port (switch) a Prepaid number to Vodacom?
Answer5: Visit a Vodacom Shop with your ID or passport and proof of residence. Or, if your current SIM is RICA-registered, SMS 'portme' followed by your new Vodacom Prepaid SIM number (e.g. 'portme 1234567789808') to 0827678287. Wait for confirmation SMS.
Source5: https://www.vodacom.co.za/vodacom/help/switch-to-vodacom

Question6: Will it take the same amount of time to switch when I initiate a port via SMS?
Answer6: Yes. If approved today, your Prepaid number will be ported between 19:30 and 23:30 tonight — except on weekends and public holidays.
Source6: https://www.vodacom.co.za/vodacom/help/switch-to-vodacom

Question7: What is the difference between the My Vodacom App and VodaPay?
Answer7: My Vodacom App was for checking balances and buying bundles. VodaPay is a full Super App: it includes those features plus money transfers, bill payments, shopping, and digital lifestyle management. Vodacom recommends switching to VodaPay.
Source7: https://www.vodacom.co.za/vodacom/help/vodapay-app-support

Question8: How do I register for VodaPay?

Question9: Why is the VodaPay app not opening or crashing?
